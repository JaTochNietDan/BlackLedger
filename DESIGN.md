# Black Ledger — prototype contract

Single-player. A new person arrives in a persistent fictional port city. Death ends their run; the city and surviving organizations remain. No inherited player assets. No multiplayer, free-form construction, tactical combat, character physics, or real-time economic clock.

Time advances only on committed actions. Reading, inspecting, and waiting for AI consume no game time. Travel visuals illustrate committed state and can be skipped. Incidents interrupt at defined opportunities; hidden plots are not exposed by the public API. The world records the outcome before presentation. SQLite transactions and action IDs prevent accidental rerolls or duplicate payments.

First campaign: rented room → paid jobs and contacts → trusted associate → racket/business → better housing → politically consequential rivalry. Two initial rival families. A small illustrated neighborhood expands to adjacent districts as influence grows. Income, housing expenses, delegated tasks and rival plans advance with action time.

AI proposes typed situations and speaks through persistent named characters. Rules determine eligibility, costs and mechanical results. Model prose must respect immutable facts; proposals never execute code or arbitrary state patches. Slow/unavailable models have authored fallback situations. AI status is inspectable. Speech is optional, uses the existing local synthesis service, and cancels on every decision or scene change.

First validation: complete a business progression, provoke a powerful casino owner, exercise warning/intervention/unaware attack, die and start as a stranger in the changed city. Refresh during decisions and repeat action IDs without duplicate consequences. Test browser at desktop and compact sizes, text-only failure states, keyboard focus, saved-game restart, and rejected AI output.

Later: more districts and businesses, richer diplomacy and investigations, staff at residences, unique commissioned scene art. Legacy/succession and marriage/children are explicitly deferred.
