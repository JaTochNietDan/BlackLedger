BLACK LEDGER — development preview

Extract the entire archive before launching.
Windows: double-click BlackLedger.exe in the extracted folder.
macOS: open Black Ledger.app (you can move the app to Applications).
Linux: open the BlackLedger executable in the extracted folder.
Keep the supporting files together on Windows and Linux.
The game opens in its own desktop window; no terminal or web browser is needed.
Close the window or choose Game > Quit to stop the game and its background server.
A second launch focuses the existing window. A working graphics driver is required.

Saves are automatic and survive replacing the extracted game folder:
Windows: %AppData%\BlackLedger\campaign.sqlite3
macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/BlackLedger/campaign.sqlite3
Linux: $XDG_CONFIG_HOME/BlackLedger/campaign.sqlite3
       (normally ~/.config/BlackLedger/campaign.sqlite3)
Stop the game before copying/backing up the save, including any -wal/-shm files.
Developer-checkout saves are not automatically imported or overwritten.
The optional BLACK_LEDGER_DB environment variable selects an explicit save.
The desktop app picks a free local port automatically.

No Go, Node, Python, account, AI model, or separate browser is needed to play.
The app includes Electron/Chromium and the Go game server.
On first launch, choose Download and enable AI for automatic local director and
voice setup. It downloads about 10–12 GB; allow extra installation space and
substantial system memory. No model weights are in the app archive.
Downloads are verified and can resume after interruption. After setup, AI works
offline. Choose Play without AI to skip, or Game > AI setup to enable/retry later.
AI files are kept in the ai folder beside the default save and survive app updates.
Quitting the app stops its local AI services as well as the game server.

These builds are currently unsigned and macOS builds are not notarized.
Your OS may block an unrecognized downloaded application. Source builds remain
available; never disable system-wide security protections to run this game.
