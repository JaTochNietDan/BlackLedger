A city with more to say

Install local AI to generate new encounters and give the city's characters their voices. Stories and speech run on your computer; no account or subscription is needed.

The first setup downloads about 10–12 GB. Allow about 20 GB of free disk space for installation. The story model needs substantial memory; generation can be slow on older computers.

Internet is needed for setup. Once installed, AI works offline.

Downloads come from GitHub, Ollama and Hugging Face. Closing setup keeps partial downloads for retry. You can reopen this screen from Game → AI setup. Authored encounters remain playable without AI.
