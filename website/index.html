01 / THE REASON WE BUILT IT

A world with rules.
Room for improvisation.

Black Ledger exists to explore an idea: an AI director that can turn the state of a living game world into a story worth playing.

Your contacts, past dealings and the places you can reach give the director something to work with. It proposes new encounters. The simulation checks them, sets the stakes, and remembers what you do.

01

The city gives context.

People have relationships. Businesses have owners. Your campaign has a history. The director draws on that world.

02

The director proposes.

A new job. A difficult conversation. A different approach. Locally generated encounters give familiar streets new possibilities.

03

You make the decision.

Time, money, respect and police attention are real game rules. Choose your approach and live with the result.

The experiment is still evolving. Today, the director creates courier, collection and mediation encounters. Narrative coherence is an active area of development.

03 / WELCOME TO BELLWETHER

Start with a room.
Build something worth losing.

The clock advances with your actions. Take your time planning the next move. The city will be waiting.

A name on the street.

Find work, build trust and earn respect. Buy property and businesses. Recruit associates and send your crew to collect, guard, repair or take risks on your behalf.

Nothing comes without a cost.

Negotiate with the families. Manage income, stock and security. Debts, raids and retaliation can turn yesterday's opportunity into today's problem.

Another life. The same city.

Death is permanent for your character. Begin a new life in a city shaped by the people, property and history that came before you.
